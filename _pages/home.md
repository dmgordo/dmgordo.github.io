I am an emeritus researcher at the Center for Communications Research--La
Jolla. My research interests include combinatorial design theory and
computational number theory.

From 1996 until 2026 I maintained the [La Jolla Combinatorics
Repository](/ljcr/), a collection of combinatorial databases that started with
covering designs, and over the years added difference sets, circulant weighing
matrices, and recently relative and signed difference sets.  These were
presented first as HTML pages, then as a MySQL database, and finally as json files
with python scripts to access the data.  This website has details about each
combinatorial object, and a link to the permanent data repository.

See my [publications](/publications/) for more information.
